**How to read a torture citation.** A torture seed citation means the oracle in `torturelib/oracle.py` checked the recovered state against every permitted world:

- every ACKed op is present;
- each op that was in flight is wholly present or wholly absent;
- rejected ops are absent;
- `check` reports `OK` after recovery.

**Design note for the A and B rows.** The engine never writes ops one at a time. A `put`, `delete` or `batch` is a single frame built in memory by `format.encode_frame` and written by one `_pwrite_all` call (`_write` line 212). The frame's `len` and `crc` fields (`len u64 | crc u32`, covering the length and the payload) are the commit marker. They sit at the **start** of the frame, but they are valid only once every payload byte is present.
